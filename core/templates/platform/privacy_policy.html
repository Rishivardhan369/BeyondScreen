Privacy & data usage

BeyondScreen stores the structured activity you submit: recorded screen time, app-duration aggregates, optional pickup, notification-count, session and timing metrics, Goal DNA, recommendations, outcomes and progress. Device sync is optional and requires explicit consent.

Not collected

BeyondScreen does not request or store notification bodies, messages, contacts, keystrokes, photos, location, or browser history. Raw OCR text and uploaded reports are processed transiently and are not retained by the application.

History and deletion

Historical assessments and Goal Rescue recommendations are frozen so they reflect what was known at the time. Reports remain until you delete them or delete your account. Device revocation stops future sync while preserving history unless you separately delete data attributed to that device.

Exports and operations

Your personal export excludes credentials and sessions. Expired pairing codes and old read notifications may be removed by maintenance jobs; user report history is not silently purged.
